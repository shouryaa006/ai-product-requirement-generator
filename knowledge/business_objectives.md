# Business Objectives and Success Metrics

Every software product must align with strategic business objectives. Objectives define *where* the product wants to go, while success metrics (KPIs) measure *how* successfully the product is getting there.

## Defining Clear Objectives

Business objectives should be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

- **Bad Objective**: "We want to get a lot of students using our app."
- **Good Objective**: "Acquire 500 active weekly users within the first three months of launch on the university campus."

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPIs are specific metrics used to evaluate the product's performance.

### 1. Growth & Acquisition
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**: The total number of unique users who perform at least one session within a calendar month.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Total sales/marketing cost spent to acquire a single paying customer.

### 2. Engagement & Retention
- **Weekly Session Frequency**: Average number of times a user opens the app per week.
- **Retention Rate (D7 / D30)**: Percentage of users who return to the app 7 days or 30 days after signup.

### 3. Financial & Revenue
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Predictable revenue generated from subscription plans.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Average transaction amount, such as tutor booking fees.

### 4. Customer Satisfaction
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: A metric ranging from -100 to +100 indicating user willingness to recommend the app to friends.
- **Tutor Rating Average**: Overall average star rating of completed sessions.
